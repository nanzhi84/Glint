“Structured concurrency binds the lifetime of concurrent tasks to a lexical scope, preventing task leaks and propagating cancellation automatically.”

核心概念拆解：结构化并发 (Structured Concurrency) Concept Breakdown: Structured Concurrency

通俗理解：就像函数作用域严格管理局部变量一样，结构化并发要求所有派生出的异步子任务都在当前代码块内闭合完成，天然杜绝后台任务泄漏并自动向下传播取消信号。

In plain words: Just as lexical scope cleanly bounds local variables, structured concurrency ensures derived child tasks finish within their enclosing scope—preventing silent task leaks and propagating cancellation safely.

安装引导 Installation Guide

由于 Glint 未通过 Mac App Store 分发，首次打开可能会触发系统安全提示。只需简单三步即可正常启用。 Since Glint is distributed outside the Mac App Store, macOS may show a security prompt on first launch. Follow three simple steps to open it safely.

1 第一步：遇到安全提示 Step 1: See the Security Warning

下载 Glint.dmg 并拖入「应用程序」。首次打开若提示「无法验证开发者」，先点击「完成」，或在访达中右键点击图标选择「打开」。 Drag Glint into Applications. When macOS displays a "can't verify the developer" warning on first launch, click "Done" or right-click the app icon and choose "Open".

2 第二步：在「隐私与安全性」中点击「仍要打开」 Step 2: Click "Open Anyway" in Privacy & Security

前往 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性，向下滑动至「安全性」区域，点击 Glint 提示旁的「仍要打开」并在弹出框中确认即可（仅首次需要）。 Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, click "Open Anyway" next to Glint, and confirm once.

3 第三步：开启「辅助功能」与「屏幕录制」权限 Step 3: Grant Permissions & Enter API Key

点击菜单栏 Glint 图标 →「设置…」，在「权限」页开启「辅助功能」与「屏幕录制」，并在「服务商」页填入 API Key 保存即可。 Click the menu bar Glint icon → Settings…, enable Accessibility and Screen Recording under Permissions, and save your API Key under Providers.
